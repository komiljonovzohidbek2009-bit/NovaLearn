Frequently asked questions

Is NovaLearn free?

The learning tools in this build are free to use. No payment is requested.

Do I need an account?

Not in this build. Accounts are not configured yet, so favorites, saved words and progress stay in your current browser page and reset when you reload.

Are the translations verified?

Uzbek translations are marked “Draft” until a qualified reviewer confirms them. Entries without a translation say so instead of guessing.

Are the IELTS materials official?

No. NovaLearn is not affiliated with IELTS, IDP Education, the British Council or Cambridge. The practice prompts were written for NovaLearn and are not real exam questions.

Does pronunciation need an internet connection?

Audio uses the speech voices built into your browser and device. The available voices depend on your device.

      About

      About NovaLearn

      Our mission is to make high-quality educational resources accessible to everyone, regardless of financial background or location.

      Who we are

      NovaLearn is an independent educational initiative. It provides free learning resources, especially in English language learning, vocabulary, grammar, IELTS preparation, technology and digital skills, and general knowledge.

      Content and accuracy

      • Definitions and example sentences in this build are written for the sample. They have not yet been checked against a licensed dictionary.
      • Uzbek translations are labeled “Draft” until a qualified reviewer confirms them. Entries without a translation say so.
      • Quiz and practice questions are original and labeled by type. They are not official exam questions.

      Privacy

      In this build, favorites, searches, saved words and progress are kept only in the open page. Nothing you enter is sent to a server. The only external request is the Google Fonts stylesheet used for typography. Pronunciation uses the speech voices built into your browser.

      Not included in this build

      • User accounts, sign-in and password recovery
      • A server database and persistent progress
      • The admin dashboard and content management
      • A contact form, a feedback system and a Help Center
      • A live dictionary API and full word coverage
      • A sitemap, robots.txt and Google Analytics
